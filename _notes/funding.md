---
title: Funding — the crowded-room fee
description: Perpetual funding rate explained with a simple analogy.
---
A perpetual futures contract has no expiry, so exchanges charge a small fee between longs and shorts to keep price near spot. Positive funding means longs pay shorts; negative means shorts pay longs.

Tiny positive funding (+0.000087 in our snapshot) is normal. The problem is not the fee — it's what it sits next to: **74.6% of positions are long**. A crowded room with one door.

We use funding as context, never as a trigger.
