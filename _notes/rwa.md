RWA stands for **real-world assets**: things that exist outside a blockchain — Treasury bills, credit, bonds, invoices, property — represented as tokens on a chain.

Think of it as a **receipt with a barcode**. The barcode lives on-chain and moves quickly; the thing it points to (a government bond, say) still lives in the normal financial world.

The number we track is **RWA TVL** — the total value locked in those tokenised-asset protocols. Our latest snapshot: **$2.845B across 191 protocols**. That tells us how big the corner of the market is, not where price goes next.

Why people watch it: it is one of the few crypto narratives tied to conventional finance, so it gets attention in both worlds. That makes it a **traffic topic**, not an automatic edge. Hype can arrive and leave while the underlying receipts stay the same.

We use RWA TVL as one quiet line in the five-measurement board. Trading decisions still come from [our written rules](/methodology/), never from a narrative.
