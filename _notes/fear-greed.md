---
title: Fear & Greed — a mood ring, not a compass
description: Crypto Fear and Greed index explained and why it can disagree with positioning.
---
The Fear & Greed Index compresses several market signals into one number from 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed). Our snapshot: **13 — Extreme Fear**.

A mood ring is fun; it isn't a map. In the same snapshot, 74.6% of derivatives positions were long — fearful people still leaning one way. That mismatch is more interesting than either number alone.

We log the mood, then wait for price and risk rules to agree.
