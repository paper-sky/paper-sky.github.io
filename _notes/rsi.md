---
title: RSI — the oversold trap
description: What RSI actually says, in plain English, and why oversold is not a buy button.
---
RSI is a speedometer for price changes, scaled 0–100. Above 70 people call it “overbought”; below 30 “oversold”. That's it — it measures how fast, not which way.

Our 4H RSI at 29.7 means selling has been fast. A fast downhill day can be followed by another fast downhill day. So we treat oversold as **a condition worth watching**, never an entry by itself.

The entry needs a trigger: a 4H close back above the EMA20, with VIX and positioning agreeing.
