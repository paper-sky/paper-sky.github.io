---
title: DXY — the dollar backpack
description: What the dollar index means for risk assets, explained simply.
---
DXY measures the dollar against a basket of other big currencies. When it rises, a dollar is stronger.

Think of risk assets carrying a backpack. A stronger dollar and higher Treasury yields make the backpack heavier — Bitcoin and stocks often feel it. Our snapshot has DXY 103.68 and US 10Y at 4.85%.

That's a headwind, not a forecast. We use it to size caution, not to predict price.
