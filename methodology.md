<h1>Methodology</h1>
<p>Paper Sky is an experiment journal. Every number comes from a public, keyless source; every decision comes from written rules. Nothing here is a recommendation.</p>

<h2>Data sources</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Macro:</strong> Yahoo Finance (DXY, US 10Y, gold, SPX/NDX, Brent), FRED.</li>
  <li><strong>Market:</strong> CoinGecko, Bitget funding and open interest, Binance long/short and taker ratios.</li>
  <li><strong>On-chain:</strong> DeFiLlama TVL and RWA category; public EVM data (gas, wallets).</li>
  <li><strong>Derivatives:</strong> Hyperliquid public info endpoint (candles, funding, book).</li>
  <li><strong>Event odds:</strong> Polymarket public APIs — context only, never a trigger.</li>
  <li><strong>News:</strong> public RSS feeds and web search, presented as neutral facts.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Risk rules (hard)</h2>
<ol>
  <li>Paper bankroll: <strong>$1,000</strong>. Risk per trade: <strong>1% = $10</strong>.</li>
  <li>Maximum drawdown 10% ($100) → pause and audit.</li>
  <li>Three consecutive losses → half size until review.</li>
  <li>Wait is a legal, logged outcome. No trade without a trigger and an entry in the ledger.</li>
  <li>No leverage, no averaging down, no martingale.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="cookies">Cookies</h2>
<p>We use a few cookies for analytics (the shared sok.lv GA4 stream, tag <code>G‑TJ0Y7GCCC3</code>). Analytics storage stays denied until you press “Accept”; “Essential only” keeps it off. The choice is saved in <code>ps_cookie_consent</code> in localStorage, and the Google tag script is only loaded after consent. If you make no choice, the compact notice hides itself after 12 seconds — that is <strong>not</strong> consent: analytics stays blocked. Reopen it any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.</p>
